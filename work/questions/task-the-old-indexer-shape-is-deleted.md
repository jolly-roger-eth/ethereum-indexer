>   (b) Stop discarding IN PLACE: a reconfigure builds a NEW generation over the same stream instead of clearing the fold. This is the only reading that is a behaviour change and that matches "the published verdict is what the verbs act on" (the verdict's state/stream split is precisely what decides whether the successor shares the stream). >   (c) Delete the BROWSER-side re-seed (`if (outcome.stateDiscarded ...) setState(currentEmptyState())` at the three sites above), which becomes redundant once the old call shape goes, since `Indexer.updateIndexer`/`updateProcessor` already drop `entry.published`/`hasPublished` (`container.ts:551, 591`) and the indirect handle has stable identity. This IS in fence and IS real cleanup — but it is ZERO behaviour change and would leave "the one thing that must not slip" delivering nothing, while "the published verdict is what the verbs act on" stays unexplained.
